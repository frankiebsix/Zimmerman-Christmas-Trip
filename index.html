EPCOT Food & Wine + Holiday Food Watch

Food plan for November 20–25.

Important festival timing

The 2026 EPCOT International Food & Wine Festival is officially listed for August 27 through November 21, 2026. That means it should be active for the first two days of this trip only: Friday, November 20 and Saturday, November 21. After that, do not count on Food & Wine booths being open.

Disney says the final 2026 marketplace menus and opening dates are still coming later, so this page is built as a smart starter plan instead of pretending the full booth list is known.

Best strategy

  • Use EPCOT on Nov. 20 or Nov. 21 if Food & Wine matters to the group.
  • Saturday, Nov. 21 is the closing day, so go earlier in the day if you want shorter booth lines.
  • For Nov. 22–25, shift the food focus to Christmas snacks, resort dining, Magic Kingdom holiday treats, Disney Springs, and whatever holiday food Disney announces closer to the trip.
  • Gluten-free note: use Disney’s allergy binder/Cast Member confirmation at each booth because festival menus and prep can change.
EPCOT • Nov. 20–21 only

Food & Wine Must-Check Items

Returning Sneak Peek

  1. Street Corn-style Dumplings from Gyozas of the Galaxy
  2. Grilled Bushberry-spiced Shrimp Skewer from Australia
  3. Kirschwasser Torte from The Alps

Gluten-Free Plan

Wait for Disney’s full 2026 Foodie Guide, then mark booth items that are listed as gluten-friendly. Until then, shrimp, grilled meats, rice bowls, and naturally gluten-free desserts are the first places to investigate.

These are the only specific 2026 returning items Disney has previewed so far. Final menus are not fully released yet.
